Welcome to the 3D design prototype

This is an interactive model of the Smart Hazard Protection System: helmet, respirator, neck cover, hearing protection, electronics and battery. It is a concept model, not a certified product.

1Look around. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, hold Shift (or two fingers) to move.
2Jump to an area. Use the round buttons at the bottom: Overview, See inside, Respirator, Hearing, Battery and more.
3Tap any part. A plain-language description, size and status appear in the Details panel.

Other tabs at the top show power and battery runtime, the NFC workflow, diagrams, drawings and the parts list.
